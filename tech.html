ANALYSIS OF MARKET PAIN POINTS

Traditional methods of keratin production are limited by outdated technologies. Here are the key challenges facing modern industries.

ETHICAL INSTABILITY

100% reliance on animal-derived raw materials (feathers, horns) creates ethical risks and threats to supply stability for premium beauty brands.

ECONOMIC INEFFICIENCY

Traditional acid extraction requires enormous energy consumption, accounting for up to 30% of the final product's production cost.

LOST PROTEIN STRUCTURE

Industrial processing leads to denaturation, transforming valuable raw material into an ineffective hydrolysate with a disrupted tertiary structure.

BIOPRODUCTION OF KERATIN

The MicBioMate biosynthesis system utilizes a traditional fermentation technology based on the controlled production of human keratins. Our architecture eliminates the use of animal-derived raw materials, replacing them with optimized microbial strains featuring a specific molecular profile.
